BLOCKR is a lightweight Chrome extension designed to block YouTube ads, common web advertising, trackers and intrusive page clutter while keeping controls simple.

What BLOCKR does

YouTube protectionHandles common pre-roll, mid-roll and promoted ad clutter.
Web protectionBlocks common advertising and tracking requests across the web.
Element ZapperHide annoying page elements and remember the choice for that site.
Privacy firstNo paid advertiser whitelist and no sale of browsing data.
